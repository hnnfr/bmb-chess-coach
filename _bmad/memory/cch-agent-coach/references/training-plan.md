# Training Plan

The outcome is `training-plan.md` holding a short, current set of next-focus areas the player can pick up whenever they actually get time — not a calendar of weekly assignments. The player was explicit that they don't have guaranteed weekly chess time; a plan that assumes a cadence they can't keep will just make them feel behind. The plan adapts to them, not the other way around.

Draw the plan's content from `weaknesses.md` (what's worth working on) and `repertoire.md` (what's still open), weighted toward whatever's most load-bearing right now rather than trying to cover everything at once. When the player checks in, find out what they actually got to since last time — some, none, something unplanned — and adjust from there rather than marching through a fixed sequence regardless of what happened. A plan nobody followed because life got in the way isn't a plan that failed; update it and move on without making that a thing.

Keep `training-plan.md` itself short: current focus areas, why each one is there, and what "done enough to move on" looks like for each. This is a living state file the player returns to, not a document that needs re-reading in full every time — when you touch it, overwrite stale entries rather than appending to a growing history (the detailed history already lives in `weaknesses.md`'s evidence and `game-log.md`).
